# Attribution and Use Terms

SolarOps was designed, built, and trained by a team of **five builders**. This file
sets out the credit that must accompany any use of the platform.

---

## Required Credit

Any use, demonstration, submission, or presentation of SolarOps — in whole or in
part — **must visibly credit the five builders of the SolarOps team**.

This applies to the platform as a whole and to its parts, including but not
limited to:

- the trained YOLOv9 detection and segmentation models,
- the normal (RGB) and thermal inference pipelines,
- the backend API and database schema,
- the frontend application,
- the training notebooks and dataset preparation work.

---

## Competition Use Is Prohibited Without Credit

**Using this platform in any competition, hackathon, contest, exhibition, or
judged event without crediting the five builders is prohibited.**

This includes, without limitation:

- submitting SolarOps or any derivative of it as your own work;
- entering it under a different team, individual, or project name;
- presenting its outputs, screenshots, metrics, or demos without attribution;
- reusing its models, code, or notebooks inside another competition entry
  without naming the SolarOps team as their source.

Entering any of the above without credit is a misrepresentation of authorship.

---

## How to Credit

Include the following notice in your README, slide deck, demo page, or
submission materials:

> Built on **SolarOps**, created by the five builders of the SolarOps team.
> Source: https://github.com/Lennah32/SOLAROPS

Keep this `ATTRIBUTION.md` file intact in any copy, fork, or derivative of the
repository.
